Good to Know

Terms & Conditions

The Black Friday offer applies to one new enrollment per student in a participating January group music class, dance class, theatre class, or tutoring pod. The first month is $49, and the standard $60 enrollment fee is waived.

Enrollment requires a commitment to the first three consecutive months. Regular monthly tuition begins in month two. This special rate is our way of helping your family get started with a full season of learning and growth. If you cancel before April 1, 2027, the waived $60 enrollment fee and the difference between your $49 promotional month and the program's regular monthly tuition will be due in full. After the initial three-month commitment, enrollment continues month-to-month under Tamraloo Studios' regular tuition, cancellation, and withdrawal policies.

Offer does not apply to current class enrollments, private lessons, camps, productions, or band programs advertised separately. It is nontransferable and cannot be combined with another introductory offer.

Classes and tutoring pods are subject to availability and minimum enrollment. Tutoring interest form submission does not reserve a space or complete enrollment. Tutoring billing begins only after a compatible pod of at least three students is confirmed and the family has completed enrollment.

If Tamraloo cannot place a student or a selected class does not launch, any promotional payment collected may be transferred to another eligible program or refunded. The early cancellation charges above do not apply when Tamraloo cannot provide the program.
